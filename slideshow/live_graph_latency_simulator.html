Measure the real number

This is a model. To get the true figure for your build, stamp a sample at acquisition and again when its point is drawn, then take the difference:

Backend: attach t_acq = time.perf_counter() to each reading before it leaves Python.
Frontend: in the draw callback, record performance.now() and compare to the sample's acquisition stamp passed alongside the data.
Report the median and 95th-percentile gap over a 30-60 s run. The 95th percentile is the honest worst-case to quote, not the average.

Safety note that outranks all of this: spike and force-limit cutoffs must run on the raw sample stream in the backend, never on what the graph shows. Display lag is a UX number; it must not sit in the safety path.

Model assumes a single-threaded UI path and averages each waiting stage over its interval (a sample waits, on average, half a sample period and half a frame). Real systems add OS scheduling jitter, USB driver buffering, and garbage-collection pauses, so treat these as a floor for typical lag, not a ceiling.
